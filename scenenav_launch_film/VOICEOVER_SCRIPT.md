# SceneNav AI — 65-Second Narration

**Voice direction:** Deep, calm documentary delivery. Natural and precise; never theatrical. Leave short breaths at em dashes and between listed stages.

| Time | Narration |
|---|---|
| 00–08s | When positioning signals disappear, the Earth does not. Its visible structure remains. |
| 08–17s | SceneNav AI compares two aerial views of the same terrain: different scale, orientation, and time. What remains is visual evidence. |
| 17–29s | ORB and SIFT find distinctive keypoints. Candidate correspondences are tested. RANSAC discards outliers, retaining geometric agreement in image space. |
| 29–40s | A homography maps the query plane into the reference raster, revealing a four-corner footprint in pixel space. |
| 40–50s | Scale, dispersion, inlier consistency, and conditioning inform reliability. The confidence index is an uncalibrated heuristic, not location probability. |
| 50–58s | Features. Matching. RANSAC. Homography. Footprint. Reliability. |
| 58–65s | SceneNav AI. Visual intelligence. Spatial understanding. A research prototype. |

The wording deliberately distinguishes image-space matching from georeferenced localization and does not claim operational navigation performance, geographic accuracy, or calibrated confidence.
